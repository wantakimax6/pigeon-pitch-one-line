A complete, original entry for the [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf) bounty on make.fun: one line, 200 characters at most, in English — the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives to investors for its startup.

## The pitch

> Invest in my startup: we've cornered the breadcrumb market, our burn rate is literally zero, and our head office is the mayor's statue.

135 characters. The program (`main.ts`) writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` in the grading sandbox).

## How it runs

```sh
node main.ts
```

Node 22 or later, no dependencies, no network, no build step.
